The dashboard reported that 44% of property viewings ended with the renter not showing up, which led to agent commissions being frozen. However, the underlying data shows a different result. Of the 200 viewings scheduled in 2023, 156 took place normally, 10 were disputed, and 34 were cancelled. Only 12 of those cancellations were renter no-shows. After excluding the 7 viewings cancelled because the property was withdrawn, the actual no-show rate is approximately 6%, significantly lower than the reported 44%.

The issue came from how the dashboard filtered the records. The calculation was supposed to exclude viewings cancelled because the property was withdrawn. However, viewings that took place have no cancellation reason recorded. Instead of including those records, the filter excluded them because the reason was missing. This removed all 166 viewings that took place and left only 27 cancellations in the calculation. As a result, the dashboard calculated the percentage of cancellations caused by renter no-shows rather than the percentage of all eligible viewings.

We recommend lifting the freeze on agent commissions since the reported 44% no-show rate was incorrect. The corrected calculation shows a rate of approximately 6%. The 10 disputed viewings involving 7 renters should be reviewed separately before releasing any related commissions. Going forward, the dashboard should also display the total number of viewings included in the calculation so similar issues can be identified earlier.
